JDT Pricing Model — Quick Guide

Standalone desktop edition. Everything is saved automatically to jdt_pricing_data.json next to the application — portable, offline, no Excel required.

1 · Set pricing assumptions

Open Settings — update driver hourly, statutory loadings, vehicle $/km, fuel levy and target margin. Everything flows from these.

2 · Enter transport lanes

On Lanes, fill the input cells: Vehicle, Spaces, Trips/wk, Hrs/trip, Km/trip (Tolls / Overnight / Load extras optional). 640 sample lanes are pre-loaded.

3 · Enter warehousing accounts

On Warehousing, fill per customer: Pallets stored, Inbound/Outbound pallets/wk, Cases/wk, VAS hrs/wk.

4 · Flag items for the quote

Tick Quote? on each lane / account to include it on the customer quote (up to 20 lanes + 10 warehousing lines).

5 · Fill customer details

On Quote, type Customer, Contact and Quote #. Date auto-fills.

6 · Generate quote PDF

Click Generate Quote PDF. The PDF is saved next to the app as JDT_Quote_<Customer>_<#>_<date>.pdf and opens automatically.

Engine

  • Loaded $/hr = base × OT × (1 + super) × (1 + workcover + payroll)
  • Labour cost = total hours × loaded $/hr
  • Fuel/veh cost = total km × vehicle $/km
  • Base cost = labour + fuel/veh + tolls + overnight + loading extras
  • Base price = base cost ÷ (1 − target margin)
  • Price inc FL = base price × (1 + fuel levy)
  • Margin % = (price − cost) ÷ price
